# 6-Day Hypertrophy Plan — PPL ×2

**Built from your split** (Chest/Shoulders → Deadlift/Back → Legs/Arms), run twice weekly. Your five requested lifts are all anchored as primary movements.

**Assumptions I made** (correct me if wrong): 1+ year of consistent training, competent technique on bench/deadlift/row, 7–9 h sleep, eating at maintenance or above. If any of those are false, this is the wrong plan — see "When to not run this" at the bottom.

---

## Schedule

| Day | Session | Anchor lift |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Push A (heavy) | **Bench Press** |
| Tue | Pull A (heavy) | **Deadlift** + **Barbell Row** |
| Wed | Legs A (quad) | Squat |
| Thu | Push B (volume) | Incline Bench + **Barbell OHP** ¹ |
| Fri | Pull B (volume) | **T-Bar Row** |
| Sat | Legs B (hamstring/glute) | RDL |
| Sun | Full rest | — |

¹ OHP appears heavy on Mon and again Thu; see note in Day 4.

Each muscle gets **72 h between sessions** — Mon/Thu, Tue/Fri, Wed/Sat. That spacing is deliberate, not incidental.

## Progression

**Double progression.** Hit the top of the rep range on *every* set at the prescribed RIR → add 2.5–5% next session. Not before.

**Log every set: load × reps × RIR.** If a lift fails to beat the prior session on load *or* reps for two consecutive sessions, that lift has stalled — deload it or swap the variation.

**Mesocycle structure:**
- Wks 1–2: Start at the RIR shown, bottom of set ranges
- Wks 3–5: Push toward 1–2 RIR, add 1–2 sets to lagging muscles if recovery allows
- Wk 6: **Deload.** Halve the sets, keep ~85% of the load, 4–5 RIR
- Repeat, rotating 2–3 accessory movements

## Execution rules

1. **Full ROM with a hard emphasis on the stretched position.** Control the eccentric (~2 s), pause briefly at length. Don't bounce.
2. **Rest longer than feels necessary.** 2–3 min on compounds. Short rest costs you reps, and reps are the currency.
3. **1–2 RIR on almost everything.** Save true failure for the last set of single-joint machine work.
4. **Never deadlift to failure.** Ever. Form degrades before the muscle does.
5. **If a session runs past 75 minutes, cut the last isolation exercise.** Don't extend.

## Support

- **Protein:** 1.6–2.2 g/kg bodyweight daily
- **Calories:** +200–400 over maintenance. Target 0.25–0.5% bodyweight gain/week. Faster is fat.
- **Carbs:** 4–6 g/kg — 6 sessions/week is glycogen-expensive
- **Sleep:** 7–9 h. This is the load-bearing variable, not the program.

---

## When to NOT run this

Drop to 4 days (Upper/Lower ×2) if **any** of these are true:
- Sleeping under 7 hours regularly
- In a calorie deficit
- Under 1 year of serious training
- Physically demanding job or high life stress
- Joint pain that persists past warm-up

Running this plan on 6 h of sleep in a deficit will produce **worse** results than a well-executed 4-day plan. That's not motivational hedging — it's the actual expected outcome.

---

## Schedule alternative

6 consecutive days with one rest day is aggressive. If you find yourself dragging by Friday, switch to a rolling **3-on / 1-off** rotation (Push, Pull, Legs, Rest — repeat). Same sessions, ~8 days per cycle instead of 7, meaningfully better recovery. It stops mapping to calendar weekdays, which is the only real downside.
